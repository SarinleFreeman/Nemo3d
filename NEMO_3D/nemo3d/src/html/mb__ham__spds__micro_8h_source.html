164/* normal order in the parameter list (15 members) for sp3s* band model
165 Notations are defined in J. Phys. Chem. Solids Vol 44, pp 365 (1983)
166 The parameters will be converted to the notation used in sp3d5s* band model
167
168 0 E(s,a) = pAs_s_s
169 1 E(p,a) = pAs_p_p
170 2 E(s,c) = pIn_s_s
171 3 E(p,c) = pIn_p_p
172 4 E(s*,a) = pAs_ss_ss
173 5 E(s*,c) = pIn_ss_ss
174 6 V(s, s) = 4 * pInAs_s_s
175 7 V(x,x) = (4/3) * (pInAs_p_p_sigm + 2* pInAs_p_p_pi)
176 8 V(x,y) = (4/3) * (pInAs_p_p_sigma - pInAs_p_p_pi)
177 9 E(sa,pc) = (4/sqrt(3)) * pInAs_sa_pc
178 10 E(sc,pa) = (4/sqrt(3)) * pInAs_sc_pa
179 11 E(s*a,pc) = (4/sqrt(3)) * pInAs_ssa_pc
180 12 E(s*c,pa) = -(4/sqrt(3)) * pInAs_ssc_pa
181 13 Spin-Orbit coupling for anion = 3 * pAs_so_so
182 14 Spin-orbit coupling for cation = 3* pIn_so_so
183*/
